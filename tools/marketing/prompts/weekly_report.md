# Step: the weekly report

You get the week's numbers from the pipeline: shifts recorded and their moments, the shorts
picked, edits written, drafts rendered, the developer's approvals, Claude's spend against the
monthly cap, the voice quota, disk use against the budget, open alerts, and platform stats when
they exist.

Write a short report for the developer, read on a phone in Telegram:
- `headline`: one line, the most important thing.
- `summary`: two to four plain sentences.
- `highlights` and `problems`: short bullets from the numbers (a spend near the cap, disk near
  the budget, few shifts recorded, drafts waiting for approval, alerts).
- `next_week`: two to four concrete suggestions (record a shift that shows the blink, approve
  the waiting drafts, try a hook that hasn't been used).
- Only what the numbers say. No platform stats yet means don't guess at views.
