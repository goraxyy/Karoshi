## Notes

- `Mumble_*` are single-syllable voice blips (0.16–0.26 s), meant to be fired
  repeatedly while an NPC "talks", not played once as a full line.
- `DoorCreak` ships 3 open + 3 close variants from the same physical door, so
  open/close stay matched.
